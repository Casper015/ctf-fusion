Root signs are signs of differences between three-repeat sample means, not adjudications of the true better action. The unweighted-root mean is +0.001830 for near and -0.000064 for far; neither replaces the primary statistic.

Near covers counts 3–15, two source maps per count. The 20 far roots cover nine counts and 12 maps; six counts have only one changed-root map, so a fixed-count within-map uncertainty interval cannot be established for that cohort. Far is separate and coverage-limited. No root was added or excluded to change the outcome.

## Intervention and continuation policy

A is the saved fixed-128, deadline-off P2 diagnostic reply. It matches the original timed recorded root reply at 330/465 near roots and 18/20 far roots; it is not universally the recorded on-policy action. B is the near/far branch-only P3 diagnostic reply, with the root P2 model unchanged. This is not full deployed-P3 versus P2 continuation.

Both alternatives use the exact same frozen deployed C08 P2/V-off binary from t+1 onward, with original opponents live and reactive. Fresh bots receive the complete recorded input prefix. The engine uses recorded orders through t-1; opponents' t replies are common and recorded, while our complete t reply is forced to A or B. All seats are live afterward through turn 400. The stock branch protocol retains bot memory of its computed root choice rather than the forced action; this limitation applies to both candidates. Two near pairs have identical one-step public successors under recorded opponents and are retained; this check is not exhaustive canonical-action equivalence.

Master 20261260 binds scheduling and candidate/repeat order. Original map and flag seeds are retained to reproduce the root and subsequent engine stream. These are three runtime/strategy repeats, not independent flag-seed replications or fresh independent maps. No variance of 0.15 was assumed.

## Repeat variation and replay diagnostics

Near: 483/930 candidate sets have three identical final utilities; 447 vary. Mean within-action sample variance is 0.00882569 for A and 0.00934458 for B. Mean sample variance of paired repeat differences is 0.02035692. Repeats share the original engine flag stream; these descriptive variances do not establish IID noise.

There are 13,429 computed-versus-recorded prefix reply mismatches across 4,965,018 prefix-seat comparisons. Recorded engine prefixes remain exact because computed prefix replies are discarded. Mismatches measure replay/history/runtime divergence and are not removed from the utility denominator.

## Validation and interpretation

The collection completed all 2,898 continuations. Independent audit re-stepped all 1,159,200 TURN blocks and checked the recorded prefix, root, forced own order, common opponent root orders, live used orders, final score vectors, utility, raw receipts and source/binary/engine/limits identities. All-phase faults: 0; prefix 0, discarded computed-root 0, live 0. Original 26-stream / 10,400-TURN / all-seat INIT / final-score preflight also passed. Bootstrap vectorized and separate loop arithmetic agree within 1e-14.

The near interval uses 5,000 predeclared percentile bootstrap draws, resampling two maps with replacement within each of 13 counts, retaining all roots of selected maps, then weighting the 13 within-count root means equally. There are 26 source maps, not 465 independent maps. The final fixed-endpoint interval is an ordinary interval; interim peeks were descriptive, no sequential significance procedure was used, and neither the endpoint nor configuration changed. Source selection is from T0013 maps, so this experiment is not independent of that full-game screen.

The evidence supports **advantage not shown** for these changed actions under the common P2 continuation policy. It does not identify deadline tax, saturation or search misuse as the unique mechanism, prove action-level equivalence, approve P3 deployment, or establish an actor route's superiority. No production default, training, export, extra game endpoint or v12 measurement changed.
