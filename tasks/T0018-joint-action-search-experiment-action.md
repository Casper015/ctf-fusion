## Goal

Code-only search experiment on the existing trained models (no retraining, no feature change, same depth and scoring; V off as the frozen heuristic baseline), kept apart from T0016 (no edits under training/ or tests/). In the family carriers generated by bots/p_only/tools/derive/make_families.py, every change behind its own build setting and the current search as the default arm: (1) a bounded per-movement action portfolio (k-best complete assignments from the existing 8-mask DP: alternate targets, attack vs pickup, explicit wait vs pickup, gated own drops) for a small movement shortlist plus the incumbent, deduplicated by complete order; audit and fix moveIndex-only equality checks and wait/pickup serialization; (2) for C08, ply-2 predictions from each candidate's own settled successor instead of the reference plan's, a diagnostic mode comparing both, exact-state caches and counters (v12 is verified, not changed); (3) bots/p_only/tools/branch_portfolio.py on top of training.branch_collect: two-unit, target, pickup/wait, drop and transport candidates with their sources, explicit carrier-arm selection with binary verification, another arm's reply as a candidate, summary counts. Contract tests, profiles, docs/SEARCH_PORTFOLIO.md. Host evaluation is a separate dependent task.
