## Goal

Measure whether the already trained designated-seat V3-XL (inference/models_pxxl_v3xl) improves the complete C08 bot over V off and the existing V2-M on unseen maps, before running the separate PR #10 search experiment. This is a host screening task, not retraining or final production confirmation. PR #9 adds infrastructure, not a new checkpoint; identify the actual model being tested. Keep T0010/T0012/T0014 and the T0016 5XL/student capacity study separate.

1. Freeze the experiment before play. Start an owned worktree from current main and record its commit, compiler/flags, engine, opponent binaries, source/model manifests, and all candidate bundle/binary SHA-256 hashes. Use existing arms c08_ponly_pxxl_v3xl (V off) and c08_ponly_pxxl_v3xl_v (new V3-XL), both on the family carrier c08_ponly_f.hpp, plus c08_ponly_pxxl_vm (V off) and c08_ponly_pxxl_vm_v (old V2-M), both on the legacy production carrier c08_ponly.hpp, and an identical-binary A/A copy of the new V arm. There is no family-carrier V2-M wrapper, so the legacy V-off arm is included as the V2-M arm's own matched control and as a carrier control. Prove every policy array is the same frozen p2 XXL model (checked 2026-10-11 at 72a3740: all 880 policy arrays of inference/models_pxxl_v3xl and inference/models_pxxl_vm equal inference/models_xxl). Verify family-carrier versus legacy-carrier equivalence with V off at fixed work (bots/p_only/tests/test_search_portfolio.py test_defaults_reply_as_production), and explicitly verify PR #10 portfolio and per-branch ply-2 settings are off (PONLY_PORTFOLIO_K and PONLY_PLY2_MODE at their defaults; the wrappers define neither). Fixed-work equivalence does not establish equal-time equivalence: the carrier comparison below measures it. Keep depth, coefficients (refine 50, beam 35), search budgets, V call/deadline settings, and all non-V behavior fixed and recorded. The old/new V comparison is a deployed-model comparison: their features, capacities and training data differ, so it does not isolate feature quality or capacity. If an existing wrapper cannot satisfy the controls, report the failed preflight and create a separate code task rather than touching another owner's lanes.

5. Primary outcome: equally count-weighted paired utility V3-XL minus matched V off, with 95% intervals clustered by independent map. Secondary: V2-M minus its matched V off (legacy carrier), the contrast of the two V gains (a model comparison without a carrier change), V3-XL minus V2-M (the deployed-bot comparison: model and carrier both differ), and legacy minus family carrier with V off (the carrier difference at equal time). Report all counts and the 2, 3-5, 6-10 and 11-15 bands; in the earlier matched screen every two-team fixture gave identical scores in every comparison and A/A, so state whether that holds here and, if it does, also give the primary result over counts 3-15 with a reproducible supplement, but do not promote a favourable subgroup when the overall comparison is inconclusive. Report A/A paired differences and intervals next to the primary result; examine effect size against rerun noise. Utility is the share of rivals beaten with half credit for ties, not a game win rate. Missing fixtures, incompatible identity, faults or delays must remain visible in denominators and reporting; do not selectively discard losing/faulted games. A paired interval crossing zero means 'not shown'. A wholly positive interval is a screening signal, not production approval or a fresh multi-seed confirmation.
