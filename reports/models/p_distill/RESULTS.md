## Reading

- Distillation from the 8XL teacher lowered the XXL student's supervised validation loss on every seed (-0.0134, -0.0113, -0.0100; mean -0.0116). The teacher itself is -0.0276 from the warmup mean, so the student recovers about 42% of it.
- The gap is largest early (about -0.04 after epoch 1) and narrows as the schedule anneals; part of the effect is faster learning.
- Calibrated per head, the whole gain is in the joint (movement) head; the act head is slightly worse on every seed and the target head does not move. This mirrors where the teacher itself beats XXL (joint -0.028, act -0.0008, target 0). The warmup arm reproduces T0011's P3 XXL per head.
- mixed4_8 QAT from the distilled models costs +0.0328, +0.0357, +0.0317 against their float loss, more than distillation gained. No supervised model was quantized, so whether distillation still helps after QAT is not measured here.
- These are prediction losses on T0011's validation split. They do not establish playing strength, the 50 ms budget, or out-of-distribution behaviour; those need an export and host games.
