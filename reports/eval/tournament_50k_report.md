**What these numbers are, and are not:**
- **The ratings are the run's own Plackett-Luce estimates, anchored at `baseline` = 0.** They are not the official pairwise Bradley-Terry scorer, and they cannot be reproduced from the repository: the fitter, its likelihood and tie handling, the rating scale, the count weights and the ordered match results are not committed. They carry no intervals, so the order of close ratings is unresolved.
- **Win rate, pairwise win rate and average score** are the run's per-size fields. The run did not record how `win_rate` treats a shared first place.
- **No run manifest is committed:** engine, runner, binary and model hashes, seeds, schedule and result digests, duplicate checks, and fault, late-reply and timing coverage are missing. See [What is missing](#what-is-missing).
- **P predicts what the opponents do,** inside the C08 or v12 heuristic search. It does not choose our own moves.

## Time limits

The summary records no faults, late replies or reply times; only the three replays do. Their `timeouts_50ms` field also counts the first reply, which may take up to 2 s: every bot whose later replies all stayed under 50 ms has exactly one such timeout when its first reply took over 50 ms, and none otherwise. The counts here leave the first reply out. In 2 of their 14 bot-games, later replies took over 50 ms (`c08_ponly_pxl` 6, `c08_control` 19), and the referee dropped 0 replies. Late replies were therefore recorded and still played, at least in these games: the official rule that drops a late reply was not applied, so timeouts cost no games, and compliance with the 2 s / 50 ms limits cannot be inferred for the run. The run used 16 workers, a heavier CPU load than the 6 of the P-only screens.

## What is missing

Needed from the host's run directory (task T0028) before these results can count as evidence:
- the ratings fitter and its exact command: likelihood, tie handling, scale, anchor, count weights;
- the ordered results of every game, with a digest, so the ratings can be refitted and given intervals clustered by game;
- a run manifest: engine, runner, every bot binary and model header with hashes, seeds, schedule digest, independent and duplicate game counts;
- per-game faults, dead bots, late replies and reply times (first reply, p50, p99, max), with how many games each field covers; faulted games stay in the denominators;
- tournament game ids for the replay highlights.
