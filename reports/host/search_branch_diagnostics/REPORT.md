- Parity covers only the team counts and event coverage listed; other counts and rarer event combinations are untested.
- P fidelity is measured on factual recorded states only; it says nothing about P on branched (counterfactual) states.
- The contact and override strata are oversampled tactical turns and represent only themselves; the all-roots pool mixes strata and is not a population estimate.
- Roots refused on replay are excluded, not imputed, so the analysed roots are not a random subset of the registered ones (see the exclusions by opponent and the analysed roots per count).
- A cross-fit gain that is not shown does not show zero opportunity, and a weak or unshown V ranking is not a unique cause of the T0081 search deficit.
