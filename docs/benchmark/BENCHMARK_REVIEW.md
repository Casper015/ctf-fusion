# Which heuristic modules help: review of AI_Test `ctf-benchmark-v3`

**Data:**
- **Per-game records of both tracks:**
  - track 1: 90,207 games, 9 candidates × ~10,000 fixtures;
  - C-series: 44,000 games, 22 candidates × 2,000 fixtures.
- **Method:** each variant is compared with the build it was made from, fixture by fixture. The numbers are in [BENCHMARK_TABLE.md](BENCHMARK_TABLE.md), generated by `tools/reporting/benchmark_review.py`; rerun it on newer results the same way.

**How to read the numbers:**
- **Effect size:** **pp** is the paired difference in pairwise score (the share of opponents beaten, which is what the BT rating rewards), in percentage points.
- **BT scale:** 1 pp is roughly 8–9 BT points, from the pairs whose leaderboard ratings are known.
- **Significance:** `+` / `-` means the 95% interval excludes zero; `++` / `--` means |z| > 3, which survives the number of tests made here.
- **The noise checks pass:** the two identical-code pairs (A0 vs B0; C17 vs C18) come out at +0.1 and -0.5 pp, both consistent with zero.

## Verdict

### Positive

| Module | Evidence | Where it helps |
|---|---|---|
| **Tuned weights (`W_*`)** | +1.6 pp `+` on the foundation; +1.5 pp `+` inside Omega. Two independent tests agree. | Positive at most sizes; the significant cells are 6 teams (foundation, `++`) and 10 teams (Omega, `+`) |
| **Sampled two-ply expectimax** | +1.6 pp `+` and +2.2 pp `+`: two identical copies, both significant | 3–15 teams. In duels it is neutral (-0.8). Omega already limits it to 3–15. |
| **Omega fusion vs Control_Hybrid** | +2.6 pp `++` (track 1); +1.3 pp `+` (C-series) | Track 1: 3, 5 and 10 teams `++`, 8 teams `+` |
| **Gene RISK (entropic risk)** | +1.3 pp vs Omega (95% CI [-0.00, +2.55], positive at 6 of the 7 sizes it can affect); **+2.6 pp `++` vs Control_Hybrid**, the largest gain of any Omega variant | 3, 5, 10 and 15 teams `+` vs Hybrid |
| **CFL, in duels only** | **Duels: +9.6 pp `++`** vs the foundation and +9.2 pp `++` vs Omega. It wins **all 250** duels against Baseline, including the 28 every other variant loses. At 3–15 teams: -0.7 pp vs the foundation (8 teams -3.6 `-`) and -3.1 pp `--` vs Omega. | 2 teams only |
| **Astra habits + carrier DP, at 3–15 teams** | Astra_Full vs Omega: +1.0 pp `++` at 3–15. Against Player2 v005 it is only +0.2 (not significant). | Spread over 3–15 (largest at 6 and 8 teams; no single size significant) |

### Negative

## Caveats that change how far these results reach

1. **Every size had a fixed opponent roster.**

   | Teams | Opponents of the candidate seat |
   |---:|---|
   | 2 | Baseline only |
   | 3 | Baseline, Player5 |
   | 4 | + Astra |
   | 5 | + Sol |
   | 6 | + Fable |
   | 8 | + Sonnet, Opus |
   | 10 | the 8-team roster plus a second Baseline and Player5 |
   | 15 | the same seven bots, each twice |

   Qwen, MiMo, Kimi, GLM, DeepSeek, Doubao and Player4 never played. **The duel results — CFL's 100% and habits' harm — are against Baseline alone.**
2. **The duel losses are a deterministic blind spot.** Every variant without habits loses exactly the same duel fixtures to Baseline: 129 in track 1, one seat each on 129 maps; 27–28 in the C-series. CFL's duel changes fix all of them. A deterministic, map-specific weakness of the Sol-derived duel planner is worth understanding, because it may cost games against stronger opponents too.
3. **Single games are noisy.** Identical code changes the outcome on 33–42% of fixtures, because anytime search depends on timing. With about 370 cells in the table, about 18 `+`/`-` cells are expected by chance. Trust `++`/`--` and the all-sizes column.
4. **The C-series has only 250 fixtures per size,** so a 95% interval is about ±2.5 pp per size. Smaller per-size effects can't be seen there.
5. **The leaderboard's top places are within noise.** C-series BT ratings from +336 to +354 are not distinguishable. The identical pair C17/C18 differs by 3.2 BT, and LIT (ranked 1st) is +0.1 pp vs Omega (ranked 7th).
6. **The README's composite estimate is optimistic.** It projects "Astra_Full at ≥6 teams + Omega in duels ≈ +365–370 BT". Paired data gives Astra_Full +1.0 pp over Omega at 3–15, about +7 BT overall, so about +360. That is statistically tied with Player2 v005 (+357.8).

## What to build and test next

**Best-supported composite:**
- **2 teams:** Omega's duel planner plus CFL's duel behavior, and no habits.
- **3–15 teams:** Omega + RISK + tuned weights.
- **Remove:** FLAGS and DROP.

**Paired cluster tests, on identical fixtures, with Player2 v005 as the reference:**
1. That composite vs Player2 v005 vs Omega, per size.
2. Duels against a broad roster (Astra, Sol, Fable, Player2, Player4, Player5, Opus). Does CFL's 100% against Baseline hold?
3. Search budget at 15, 20 and 30 ms on Omega + RISK, in official timing (late replies dropped). The earlier benchmarks used record mode and never penalized a timeout.
4. Astra habits + CDP on top of RISK at 6–15 teams.
5. Wider rosters at 6–15 teams (Qwen, MiMo, Kimi, GLM, DeepSeek, Player4), so results generalize beyond seven opponents.

**Consequences for ctf-fusion:**
- Habit modeling helps only at large team counts and hurts in duels. That supports [REVIEW.md](../REVIEW.md): opponent policy P first at 3–15 teams, duels untouched.
- The collection pool is already wider than these benchmark rosters ([DATA_FORMAT.md](../../training/specs/DATA_FORMAT.md)).
